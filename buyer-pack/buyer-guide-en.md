## Can foreigners buy here

Yes. Johor sets a RM1,000,000 minimum purchase price for foreign buyers, **but new strata units bought directly from the developer inside the Medini zone are exempt from that floor**, so entry here can sit below RM1 million.

Points to keep in mind:
- The exemption removes the price floor, not the approval requirement
- The approval route and its fee should be confirmed by your lawyer in writing
- These rules were adjusted in both 2025 and 2026, so reconfirm before signing

## What a foreign buyer pays (2026)

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Down payment | 30% to 40% of price, as foreign buyers are generally financed at 60% to 70% LTV |
| Transfer stamp duty (MOT) | 8% of price, raised from 4% on 1 January 2026 |
| Johor foreign consent fee | Currently 3% of price or RM30,000, whichever is higher. Confirm the actual figure with your lawyer |
| SPA and loan legal fees | Listed as covered under the developer's package. Confirm the written terms |
| Valuation and incidentals | About RM2,000 to RM4,000 |

While holding: maintenance is estimated at about RM0.32 per sq ft per month plus a 10% sinking fund, with the final rate set by the management body. Service residences may be billed at commercial rates for utilities and assessment.

On sale: real property gains tax for a foreign individual is 30% within the first five years and 10% from the sixth year onwards.

On rental: a non-resident landlord is taxed at a flat 30% on net rental income.

For Singapore buyers: CPF cannot be used for Malaysian property. If you hold an HDB flat, check the applicable restrictions with HDB or your own adviser first.

## The process

1. Select a unit, complete the booking form and pay the booking fee
2. Submit passport, proof of address and income documents
3. Sign the sale and purchase agreement about two weeks later
4. Apply for foreign purchase approval while the loan application runs in parallel. This stage takes time
5. Pay by construction stage until handover
6. Take vacant possession, inspect, and raise defects
7. Complete the transfer of title and pay the 8% stamp duty

Step 7 falls due long after booking and the amount is substantial. Set it aside from day one.

## What you should know before you decide

1. Johor holds about 9,000 unsold serviced apartment units, the largest stock in the country. Both rental and resale face competition from similar units.
2. At current rents and costs, units of this type are generally held at negative cash flow. The return case rests on capital growth, not rental income.
3. Cash needed at entry comes to roughly 45% to 50% of the purchase price.
4. Tax for foreign owners does not fall to zero with time.
5. Handover is 2027 to 2028, with construction and market uncertainty in between.
6. The RTS station is not in Medini.
7. The tenure is 99-year leasehold, not freehold.

## Common questions

**Does buying give me a visa?** No. Property ownership and residency are separate matters.

**Can I run it as an Airbnb?** No official prohibition was published as at August 2026, but the management body can set its own house rules after handover, so this cannot be guaranteed now.

**Will maintenance fees rise?** The current figure is a pre-handover estimate. The formal rate is decided by the management body.

**Is there a Bumiputera quota?** Units here carry no Bumiputera quota, and buyers of any nationality can purchase.

**Can I buy through a company?** Yes, though the tax and stamp duty treatment differs. Speak to a tax adviser first.
